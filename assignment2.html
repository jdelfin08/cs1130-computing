Tax table used (Philippines, TRAIN Law, 2023 onwards):
Up to 250,000: no tax
250,001 to 400,000: 15% of the amount over 250,000
400,001 to 800,000: 22,500 + 20% of the amount over 400,000
800,001 to 2,000,000: 102,500 + 25% of the amount over 800,000
2,000,001 to 8,000,000: 402,500 + 30% of the amount over 2,000,000
Over 8,000,000: 2,202,500 + 35% of the amount over 8,000,000
